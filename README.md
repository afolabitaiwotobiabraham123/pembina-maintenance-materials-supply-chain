# Maintenance & Materials Supply-Chain Performance Dashboard

##  Project Status

 **Project in Progress**

This project is part of my **Energy, Supply Chain & Operations Analytics Portfolio**.

The goal is to explore how data analytics can be used to understand maintenance activities, material availability, supplier performance, and operational delays.

---

##  Problem Statement

Maintenance activities can depend heavily on the availability of the right materials at the right time.

Delays in procurement, supplier delivery, inventory availability, or work-order processing may contribute to longer maintenance cycles.

The analytical question is:
**Can data analytics identify the materials, suppliers, work orders, and processes associated with maintenance and supply-chain delays?**

---

## Proposed Solution

I plan to build a:

### **Maintenance & Materials Supply-Chain Performance Dashboard**

The dashboard will provide visibility into:

* Work-order performance
* Material availability
* Procurement lead times
* Supplier delivery performance
* Maintenance delays
* Inventory shortages
* Operational KPIs

---

##  Key Analytical Questions

1. Which materials experience the most shortages?
2. Which suppliers have the longest delivery times?
3. Which work orders experience the greatest delays?
4. What percentage of maintenance activities are delayed by material availability?
5. How long does procurement take?
6. Which materials have consistently high demand?
7. Are there recurring supplier performance issues?
8. Which maintenance categories require the greatest material support?
